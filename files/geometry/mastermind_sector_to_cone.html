Arc length of a sector
Arc length=θ360×2πr
where θ = 120° and r = 21 cm
Circumference of the base
C=2πr
where r = 7 cm is the radius of the base
Arc length of the sector  =  Circumference of the base
Area of the base
A=πr2
where r = 7 cm is the radius of the base
When a sector is folded into a cone, its arc length becomes the circumference of the base.
