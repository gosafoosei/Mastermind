Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026 · Applies to mastermindsimulations.vercel.app and the offline package

The short version: we collect your name and email when you create an account, and that's it. We don't run ads, we don't plant tracking cookies, and we never sell data. Your learning progress stays in your own browser.

1. Who we are

Mastermind Simulations is an educational website operated by Team Mastermind of Mastermind Scholars World. For any privacy question or request, contact us at gosafoosei1@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

When you create an account, we collect through our authentication provider (Supabase):

  • Full name — so the site can greet you and keep sessions readable.
  • Email address — used as your login identifier and for password resets.
  • Authentication metadata — timestamps and technical records Supabase keeps to run sign-in securely.

We do not collect: payment details (the site is free), location data, contacts, photos, or anything from your device's files.

3. What stays on your device

ItemWhere it livesPurpose
Session tokenYour browser's local storageKeeps you signed in between visits
Practice/quiz progressYour browser's local storageRemembers your answered questions and scores
Lab preferencesYour browser's local storageRemembers settings like dark mode

Clearing your browser storage removes this data from your device. It is never uploaded to us.

4. Cookies & trackers

We do not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or social-media pixels. The only stored credentials are your own session token (see the table above). If we ever add optional analytics, this policy will be updated first.

5. Who processes our data

Accounts are handled by Supabase (supabase.com), which stores authentication data on our behalf in the European Union (West EU region). The site itself is served by Vercel (vercel.com). Both are established service providers with their own privacy and security commitments. We do not transfer your data to any advertiser or data broker — ever.

6. How long we keep it

Your account data is kept while your account exists. If you want your data deleted, email us and we will remove your account within 30 days. Data in your browser (progress, sessions) can be deleted by you at any time by clearing site data.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have rights over your personal data — including the right to access it, correct it, export it, or have it deleted. In Ghana these rights are protected by the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843); for visitors in the EU/EEA, the GDPR applies. To exercise any right, contact gosafoosei1@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Students and children

The site is designed for classroom use, and teachers sometimes help students create accounts. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under the applicable digital-consent age has created an account without your permission, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

All traffic runs over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed and salted by Supabase — nobody at Team Mastermind can read them. We never ask for your password by email or chat.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change anything material, we will update the date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, show a notice on the sign-in page.
