      1. Explore a region until you can name every label from sight.
      2. Read the clinical card — landmarks and attachments are the exam's favourite targets.
      3. Run Bone Hunt (Explore tab) until your streak hits double digits.
      4. Sit the Exam (70% to pass) and review every explanation you missed.
