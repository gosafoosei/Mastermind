About the project

Learning you can see, touch, and take offline

Mastermind Simulations is a suite of interactive learning labs covering physics, mathematics, computing, and biology — built by Team Mastermind of Mastermind Scholars World for classroom teaching and independent study.

3D & Interactive Labs

Drag, adjust, and experiment — projectile motion, circle geometry, binary logic, and the human skeletal system rendered in real time.

Works Without Internet

Every asset is local. Download the offline package and the full suite runs from a USB stick or a school laptop with no connection.

Practice & Theory

Each lab pairs its simulation with worked theory pages and auto-marked practice banks drawn from real past-paper questions.

Accounts, Not Ads

A free account keeps the suite available to real learners. We collect only an email and name — no advertising, no trackers, no data selling.

Who it's for

  • Students preparing for WASSCE, IGCSE, and equivalent exams who want to see the concepts move.
  • Teachers who need a dependable visual aid that works even when the school internet doesn't.
  • Schools and study groups running the offline package across shared computers.

What's inside (v1.6)

  • Physics — projectile motion lab with live trajectory controls.
  • Geometry — circle-segments lab, plane/solid area and perimeter drills.
  • Computing — binary, logic, and number-system interactives.
  • Biology — skeletal system lab with scanned theory and practical question sets.

How it's built

No frameworks, no build server, no tracking scripts — just standards-based HTML, JavaScript, and Tailwind CSS, with Supabase handling account security. That keeps the site fast, private, and small enough to run fully offline.
